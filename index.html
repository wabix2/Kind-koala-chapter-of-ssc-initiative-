About us

Mission, values & what makes us unique

Mission

To connect student leaders with community needs — through service, healthcare awareness, and environmental action.

Values

Integrity, kindness, local ownership. We believe small acts create massive change.

Origin

Founded by SSC students in 2023, now an officially recognized chapter with active programs year-round.

✨ Why join? – Benefits of being a member

Leadership training Real impact projects Certificate & recognition Active & organized club

SAVE A CHILD'S HEART ❤️

Kind Koala Ethiopia SSC Chapter & the Medical Club of SSC are partnering with Children Heart Fund Ethiopia to support children suffering from heart disease 🏥💔

Many children are still waiting for treatment their families cannot afford. Your support can change a life ✨

💥 HOW TO VOLUNTEER

📱 SMS & Telebirr donations 🏫 Class fundraising activities 📣 Social media campaigns 👥 Join individually or in groups

🎁 REWARDS & IMPACT

  • 🏅 Certificates for volunteers
  • 🏥 Possible visit to the treatment center
  • 💙 Deep satisfaction of saving a child’s life
