About the Chapter

mission · values · why we exist & what we offer

Our Mission

To create an inclusive, student-driven platform that connects empathy with action — providing free resources, mentorship, and service projects that uplift Ethiopian communities.

Core Values

Integrity, radical kindness, local ownership, and transparency. We believe small gestures create a ripple of change.

Origin Story

Founded by SSC students who wanted a safe, action-oriented club. Now a full chapter, officially recognized and expanding across Ethiopia.

Why Join Kind Koala?

Professional growth & networking

Active & well-organized club structure
